Write a story a model can follow

Go to narc.insight.uidaho.edu/narrate and enter this invite code:

AISYMP2026

Valid through November 30, 2026.

  1. Pick a puzzle. Chat is the quickest.
  2. You see every grid, including the one the model won’t see.
  3. Write two to five sentences about what happens. Don’t spell out cells, numbers or coordinates. A new setting is welcome: cooking, sports, your field.
  4. Submit. A test takes up to two minutes. If it says the model is busy, wait a minute.
  5. Read the result, revise, and try again.

Challenge: Narrate’s model failed The Accident with the original story. Can yours get it there?

Narrate is a research study for adults 18 or older, and it’s voluntary. We don’t ask for your name or email. Stories may be published without your name, so leave personal information out.
